# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach the organization on agile practices and continuous improvement. They enable the team to operate at peak effectiveness within the agile framework.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor team velocity and sprint burndown
- Escalate organizational impediments to Product Lead or Project Manager
- Foster psychological safety and encourage continuous learning

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable pace of delivery
- Optimize team processes and reduce friction
- Enable self-organization and accountability
- Improve sprint predictability and delivery quality

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members to address blockers
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Escalation summaries to Project Manager and Product Manager

### Interacts With
- **Developers**: Daily communication on blockers and sprint progress
- **Project Manager**: Escalation of organizational impediments and planning alignment
- **Product Manager**: Sprint planning and acceptance criteria clarification

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create intuitive, accessible user interfaces and validate usability. They bridge user needs with technical implementation, ensuring that products are both functional and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces and user experiences aligned with product goals
- Conduct usability testing and user research
- Create design specs, wireframes, and prototypes
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Maintain design systems and component libraries
- Validate design against accessibility standards and user feedback

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce support tickets through intuitive design
- Ensure consistent brand and interaction patterns
- Accelerate time-to-value for end users

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with product and development teams
- Usability test findings and recommendations
- Design system updates and component specs
- User feedback summaries and iteration plans

### Interacts With
- **Product Manager**: Understanding user needs and validating design decisions
- **Developers**: Collaborating on implementation and design feasibility
- **Support Specialist**: Gathering user feedback and pain points
- **Data Analyst**: Analyzing user behavior metrics to inform design iterations

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define metrics, create dashboards, and provide insights that inform product and delivery decisions. They translate raw data into actionable intelligence for the team and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics aligned with project goals
- Design and maintain dashboards for key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Analyze user behavior, feature adoption, and system performance
- Generate reports and insights for product and delivery decisions
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for optimization
- Partner with developers on instrumentation and telemetry implementation

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision making
- Measure and demonstrate product impact
- Identify performance bottlenecks and optimization opportunities
- Provide transparency into project health and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based analytics reviews
- Dashboard updates and metric definitions
- Post-release impact analysis and retrospective insights
- Trend reports and actionable recommendations

### Interacts With
- **Product Manager**: Defining metrics and validating feature success
- **Project Manager**: Tracking project health, velocity, and delivery metrics
- **Developers**: Collaborating on instrumentation and data collection
- **UX/UI Designer**: Analyzing user behavior and design impact

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads assess architectural and code-level security risks, lead incident response, and ensure compliance. They protect the product and users while enabling the team to ship securely.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security architecture reviews and threat modeling
- Perform or coordinate security code reviews
- Lead security incident triage and response
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Track and report on security posture and compliance
- Educate the team on secure coding practices and emerging threats

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and incidents
- Enable the team to ship with confidence
- Maintain compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Foster a security-conscious culture

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during design and planning phases
- Code review comments and vulnerability findings
- Incident response summaries and post-mortems
- Security dashboard and compliance reports
- Training and awareness communications

### Interacts With
- **Developers**: Security code reviews and secure coding guidance
- **Project Manager**: Security incident escalation and disclosure coordination
- **Product Manager**: Security requirements and risk-based prioritization
- **Stakeholders**: Compliance reporting and security posture updates

---

## Support Specialist

### Role Summary
Support Specialists provide customer-facing support, capture product feedback, and ensure knowledge is accessible to users. They are the voice of the customer and help close the feedback loop between users and the product team.

### Responsibilities
- Provide timely, empathetic support to end users
- Triage and categorize support tickets and issues
- Escalate bugs, feature requests, and critical issues to the development team
- Maintain and update knowledge bases and help documentation
- Gather and synthesize user feedback and pain points
- Track support metrics and trends to inform product improvements
- Assist with release communication and user training

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce support volume through self-service and preventive measures
- Accelerate bug discovery and fix cycles
- Provide product team with direct customer insights

### Typical Communication
- Daily ticket triage and escalation summaries
- Feature request and bug reports to development team
- User feedback synthesis and trends to Product Manager
- Release notes and user communication
- Support metrics and satisfaction tracking

### Interacts With
- **Developers**: Bug escalation, reproduction steps, and technical questions
- **Product Manager**: User feedback, feature requests, and product improvements
- **UX/UI Designer**: User pain points and usability issues
- **Project Manager**: Release communication and user training coordination

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When defining projects, assign clear ownership to relevant personas and clarify interaction patterns.
- Use the interaction patterns as a guide for sprint planning and stakeholder communication.
